About

I'm Viren. I think about how intelligence actually gets built — the data that feeds it and the silicon that runs it — and I like being close to the metal of both. Right now that means building RL environments and computer-use infrastructure at Chakra Labs: the worlds frontier labs train and evaluate agents in.

I got here on a winding path — DeFi and crypto research (Coinbase, Alchemy), a quant summer at Citadel, a pile of hackathon wins, and a 3D NFT project, ALCI, that turned blockchain entropy into point clouds. That splat/point-cloud aesthetic is all over this site on purpose — it's the medium I keep coming back to.

Off the clock I'm filming trips and cutting them into edits, training, and going deep on the things that don't pay the bills yet: holograms, space, the physics underneath it all — and arguing about anime. I studied MIS at UT Austin's McCombs (3.9, with CS + computational science minors), ran the MIS Association, and was VP of Texas Blockchain.

Best ways to reach me are in the footer. This is the running home base — the notes are where I think out loud, the films are where I don't.
