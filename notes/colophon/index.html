Colophon, or: why there's a point cloud staring at you

June 20, 2026 · meta

This is the first note, so it’s mostly a throat-clear. Edit or delete me — I’m a placeholder you can write over.

I wanted one place that was actually mine: not a feed, not a profile someone else owns. Somewhere to think out loud about the stuff I spend my days and nights on — post-training and RL environments, where training data really comes from, the semiconductors underneath all of it, and the occasional detour into space, holograms, and physics I half-understand.

The look isn’t an accident. The rotating thing on the home page is a real point cloud, rendered live in your browser, colored by depth — cyan near, violet far. It’s a nod to ALCI, the 3D project where I first got obsessed with gaussian splatting and turning raw entropy into something you can look at. The whole site is themed like a depth sensor reading itself out.

Under the hood it’s about as simple as it gets: static pages, built with Astro, hosted free on GitHub Pages. Notes are just markdown files — which means writing one is as low-friction as I could make it, so I actually do it.
